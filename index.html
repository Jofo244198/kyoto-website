Explore the Cultural Heart of Japan

Kyoto is home to stunning temples, peaceful gardens, traditional cuisine and centuries of history.

Top Attractions

Fushimi Inari Shrine

Famous for its thousands of red torii gates leading through forested mountain paths.

Kinkaku-ji (Golden Pavilion)

A breathtaking Zen temple covered in gold leaf, reflected in a tranquil pond.

Arashiyama Bamboo Grove

A peaceful forest walkway surrounded by towering bamboo stalks.

Why Visit Kyoto?

Kyoto offers a unique blend of traditional Japanese culture and natural beauty. Visitors can explore ancient temples, participate in tea ceremonies, enjoy seasonal festivals and experience authentic Japanese cuisine.

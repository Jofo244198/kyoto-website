Travel Tips

Best Time to Visit

The best times to visit Kyoto are during spring (March–May) for cherry blossoms and autumn (October–November) for colourful foliage.

Getting Around

Kyoto has an efficient public transport system including buses and trains. Walking and cycling are also popular ways to explore the city.

Budgeting

Visitors should budget for accommodation, transport and food. Kyoto offers options ranging from budget hostels to luxury hotels.

Travel Etiquette

Respect local customs such as removing shoes indoors, being quiet on public transport and following temple guidelines.

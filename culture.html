Culture & Traditions

Tea Ceremonies

Kyoto is famous for traditional Japanese tea ceremonies, where visitors can experience the preparation and serving of matcha in a calm and structured environment.

Geisha Culture

The Gion district is known for its geisha traditions. These highly trained entertainers preserve historic Japanese arts such as music, dance and conversation.

Festivals

Kyoto hosts many seasonal festivals, including the Gion Matsuri, one of the most famous festivals in Japan, featuring parades and cultural performances.

Traditional Food

Kaiseki Cuisine

A traditional multi-course Japanese meal focusing on seasonal ingredients and artistic presentation.

Matcha Desserts

Kyoto is known for high-quality green tea used in sweets, drinks and desserts.

Yudofu (Tofu Hot Pot)

A simple yet delicious dish popular in Kyoto, especially near temple areas.
